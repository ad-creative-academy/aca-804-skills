# Play: comment response
**What:** Opens by showing or reading a real comment, then answers it.
**Choose when:** a skeptical or provocative comment has high likes.
**Steps:** 1) Pick the comment (verbatim, with its ID). 2) On screen: the comment as a reply bubble. 3) Spoken: a calm, confident first line that answers it ("Fair. I thought that too, until day seven.").
**Good output:** feels native to the platform, not defensive.
**Check:** the comment is real, and the answer doesn't overclaim.
