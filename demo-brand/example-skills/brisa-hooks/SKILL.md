# Brisa hooks (example playbook skill)

## Goal
Take a concept (persona × angle × awareness level × ad type) and return 3 to 5 hooks that would make that persona stop, in Brisa's voice, built on real customer language.

## Before you start, read
- `when-to-use.md` (always)
- `03-research/audience/customer-voice.md`, `04-concepts/personas.md`, `01-brand/dos-and-donts.md`

## Steps
1. Confirm the concept. If the persona or awareness level is missing, ask.
2. Read `when-to-use.md` and choose one or two plays. Start your answer by saying which and why, in one line.
3. Open the chosen `plays/<name>.md` and follow it.
4. Write 3 to 5 hooks. For each: the spoken line, what's on screen in the first second, the sound (the ear stopper), the source (review or comment ID) if it uses customer words, and the play used.
5. Check: understandable with the sound off? Lands in under three seconds? Follows the do's and don'ts (no "detox", no "48-hour", no aluminum fear, no Coconut & Lime)? Visually different from each other?
6. **Show the hooks. Don't write scripts or briefs from them unless asked.**
