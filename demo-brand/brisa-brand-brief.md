# Brisa: demo brand brief (Course 804)

> **Fictional brand.** Brisa was created for Ad Creative Academy Course 804, *The Agentic Creative Strategist*. The product, founder, reviews, comments and numbers in this pack are invented for teaching. Before the Lovable site goes public, run a quick trademark and domain check on the name.

## One line

**Brisa is a refillable, aluminum-free deodorant that actually lasts until 4pm.**

## Why this brand works for the course

- **Easy to render in AI statics.** It's a simple stick in a colored case, so placing it in any static ad type is easy and the image model has less to get wrong.
- **Emotional reviews.** The "adjustment week" when people quit antiperspirant, the 4pm-meeting panic and baking-soda rashes all turn into strong hooks and copy.
- **Clear micro personas.** There are at least six, and each has a different pain point.
- **Three offer types.** Starter kit, refill subscription and a bundle, so students can build evergreen and offer-based statics.
- **Real competitors to research.** Wild, Native, Myro, Fussy, Salt of the Earth and Each & Every all run ads you can look up in the Meta Ad Library. Research to inspire, not to copy.
- **Built-in compliance lessons.** Deodorant is a cosmetic, so students practice flagging claims that need proof ("48-hour", "clinically tested"). They also learn to avoid health scares around aluminum. This makes a good QA and compliance exercise.

## Product line

| Product | Price (EUR) | Notes |

| Starter Kit: reusable case + 1 refill | $28 | Case in 3 colors: Sand, Sage, Coral. A fourth color, Ink, launches in January, so keep it off the site for now |
| Single refill | $12 | Compostable paper tube |
| Refill subscription | $10 per refill | Every 6, 8 or 10 weeks. Skip or cancel anytime |
| Refill 3-pack | $30 | The value offer used in offer statics |

**Scents:** Sea Salt & Neroli (hero), Cedar & Fig, Coconut & Lime, Unscented (Sensitive).

**Formula story (keep it simple):** aluminum-free, baking-soda-free, with magnesium and arrowroot. No white marks on black tops.

**Claims students must treat carefully:** "lasts all day" (fine as a customer quote, not as a stated fact). "48-hour protection" (needs substantiation, so flag it). Anything suggesting aluminum is dangerous (don't say it, full stop).

## Micro personas (starting set, which the Creative OS will refine)

1. **The Week-One Switcher.** She recently quit antiperspirant and is scared of the adjustment week. Needs reassurance and a plan.
2. **The Sensitive-Skin Refugee.** Got a rash from baking-soda naturals and gave up. Needs proof it's gentle.
3. **The 4pm Meeting Sweater.** Hybrid office worker with stress sweat who fears the late-afternoon smell. Needs confidence.
4. **The Gym-to-Desk Commuter.** Cycles or runs to work and showers at the office. Needs proof of performance.
5. **The Low-Waste Refiller.** Hates plastic and already refills everything else. Needs the refill story.
6. **The Ingredient Checker.** Reads every label (often pregnant or a new parent). Needs transparency, not fear.
7. **The Partner Who Stole It.** Men who started using their partner's Unscented stick. A comedy angle.

## Visual direction (for Claude Design / Lovable)

- **Feel:** Mediterranean morning. Clean, sunny, a little playful. Not clinical, not "crunchy".
- **Palette (suggested):** Sand #F2E8D8, Sea #2F6F73, Coral #F07C5A, Ink #1E2A2F, White #FFFFFF.
- **Type feel:** a friendly rounded sans for headlines, a plain sans for body.
- **Photography:** real skin, real armpits (tasteful), gym bags, office chairs, sunlight on tiles, the colored cases lined up.
- **Avoid:** leaves-and-twigs "natural" clichés and stock-photo smiles.

## Voice (brand, not Mirella)

Warm, a bit cheeky, honest about the adjustment week. Talks like a friend who's also a physio.
Sample line: "Your armpits are not broken. They're readjusting. Give it a week."

## Website pages to build in Lovable (keep it small)

1. **Home.** Hero ("Natural deodorant that makes it to 4pm"), the three reasons, reviews, starter kit CTA.
2. **Product page, Starter Kit.** Scent and case color selectors, subscription toggle, FAQ (adjustment week, sensitive skin, how refills work).
3. **"Your first week" page.** The adjustment-week guide. This page is reused in lesson 6.2 as the landing page built from a winning ad.
