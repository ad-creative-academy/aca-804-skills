# How we read the Brisa ad account

In the Creative OS this doc lives at `02-language/how-we-read-the-ad-account.md`. It follows the quick performance analysis from the research module.

"Just because an ad is running doesn't mean it's working."

## What we're trying to achieve
- North Star: starter kits that turn into a refill subscription within 60 days (currently about 31%, target 40%).
- Day to day: starter-kit CPA. Target $32. Under $28 we scale. Over $40 for 7 days or more with real spend, we pause or iterate.
- First-order ROAS is context, not the goal.

## The unicorn ad
- AD-01, the calendar ad (Week-One Switcher, creator talking head). It has disproportionate spend and staying power: launched in June and still carrying the account.
- Every other ad is compared with it. When we reverse engineer a new winner, we use the same building blocks we used on AD-01.

## The 30-day snapshot
- Look at formats, top ads by CPA and conversion rate, angles and styles.
- Retargeting is left out when we judge new concepts (for example AD-04, 30% off, $410 spend). Those people already wanted the product.
- Anything with less than about $500 in spend is an early signal at most. A little above that is still early (for example AD-08, $620).
- Discount ads are left out when we judge evergreen prospecting concepts.
- Always say the time window and the spend next to a number.

## 7 days vs 30 days
- When the last 7 days are worse than the 30-day picture, we flag fatigue.
- Frequency above 3 on a long-running winner means fatigue is coming. AD-01 is at 3.1. Plan iterations before CPA rises.

## Hook rate and hold rate
- Hook rate = 3-second views / impressions. Hold rate = ThruPlays / impressions.
- Hook rate above about 0.30 is strong for this account. Below about 0.20, work on the first three seconds.
- Hold rate above about 0.10 is healthy here.
- Good hook rate with a low hold rate: the body lost them. Reorder clips, add new shots, make it shorter.
- Good CTR with a bad CPA: check congruency with the landing page.

## Our benchmarks
Benchmarks are account-specific. The numbers in this doc are Brisa's, from Brisa's own account. They are fictional and belong to this demo only. Every account has its own.

## How we talk about results
- Say "early signal" when it is one, and "too early to call" when an ad is new.
- Link each result to the hypothesis it was testing (see `06-pipeline/creative-pipeline.md`).
