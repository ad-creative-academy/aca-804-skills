# QA checklist for statics

Check every generated image. Tag **pass / fix / fail** with one line of reason. If the team keeps its own checklist at `02-language/qa-checklist.md`, use that one and add anything from here it lacks.

## From the team QA checklist
- **Aspect ratio:** the size asked for (4:5, 9:16, 1:1), rebuilt properly for each, not cropped.
- **Safe zones:** key text is clear of the profile name at the top and the CTA bar at the bottom.
- **Brand guidelines or native fonts:** follows the brand look, or looks native on purpose, as the row says.
- **Spelling and grammar:** text is exactly as approved. No words the model added.
- **Hierarchy:** one clear focal point. The eye knows where to go first.
- **CTA matches the objective** and the offer.
- **Congruency with the landing page:** the promise, price and offer match the page the ad points to.
- **Different enough:** it doesn't look like another ad in the batch.

## Product accuracy
- Matches `01-brand/brand-assets/`: shape, color, label, logo. No warped or made-up packaging.
- Only products that are in stock and allowed.

## Leftovers from the reference brand
- No names, logos, products, people, characters or distinctive artwork from the reference.
- No wording carried over from the reference.

## Claims
- Nothing the do's and don'ts ban. No claim the brand can't prove. No health scares.
- Customer quotes match their source ID, word for word after any approved trim.
- Disclaimers present where needed (results may vary, simulated imagery, AI-generated).

## Readability and craft
- Readable on a phone at feed size. Good contrast.
- No uncanny hands, faces or objects.

A **fail** on product accuracy, reference leftovers or claims is always a fail, however good the rest looks.
