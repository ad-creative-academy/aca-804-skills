# Competitors and trends (Course 804, lesson 3.3)

These are pillars three and four of the research module. The hard rule is **research to inspire, not to copy**. Take structure and insight only. Never take their words, visuals, claims or brand assets.

## Before you start
- Read `03-research/competitors/competitor-list.md` and `04-concepts/personas.md`. If there is no list, ask which brands to cover.
- Agree the source, and say which you used:

| Source | How |
|---|---|
| Ad libraries (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Google Ads Transparency Center, Foreplay) | Through a connector, if one is set up |
| Browser | Read slowly, like a person would |
| Screenshots | The user drops them in `00-inbox/` |

With TikTok Creative Center top ads, be careful: sorting by engagement doesn't tell you what sells.

## Part 1: Per competitor
| Look at | Questions |
|---|---|
| Creative volume and budget indicators | How many ads are live? How often do new ones appear? |
| Format strategy | Video vs static, lengths, ad types |
| Talent approach | Creators, founder, studio, experts. Described by role, not personal attributes |
| Creative execution | Hooks, pacing, branded vs native, text on screen |
| Temporal patterns | What launches when, seasonal pushes, what gets refreshed |

**Long-running ads are a signal, never proof.** "Just because an ad is running doesn't mean it's working." Note them as worth studying and say what you can't see (spend, results).

## Part 2: What it means for us
1. **White space:** personas, angles, awareness levels or ad types nobody in the category is covering.
2. **How to differentiate:** where we can say or show something they can't.
3. **Proven approaches to elevate:** a structure that keeps showing up. Reverse engineer it with the building blocks, then say how we would do it better in our own words and world. Never copy.
4. **Outside the category:** one brand in another category that talks to the same persona, and what its structure teaches.

## Part 3: Trends
Sort each trend with the filter from the research module:

| Type | Lifespan |
|---|---|
| Moments | 1 to 4 weeks |
| Signals | 1 to 6 months |
| Forces | 6 months or more |

Then three steps for each trend worth using:
1. **Find:** what it is and where you saw it.
2. **Relevance:** does it fit our persona on behavioral, cultural, psychological and emotional grounds? If not, drop it.
3. **Narrative:** the hook, the structure, and where the product sits.

For each, note the hook, visuals, timing and pacing, audio and text style. The goal is to blend in and stand out.

## Output
- `03-research/competitors/<brand>.md`, one per competitor, with source, date and a summary line.
- `03-research/trends/<YYYY-MM>.md`.
- In chat: the three most useful findings, each as a candidate row for `aca-concept-matrix`, with the evidence.

This is a monthly habit. Offer to put it on the calendar as a scheduled run with a browser and read-only access.

## Never
- Copy or lightly reword a competitor's lines, visuals, claims, offers or brand assets into our work.
- Save competitor ads into the swipe file without tagging them as structure references.
- State a competitor's spend or results as fact. You can only see indicators.
- Log in, sign up, or buy anything.
