# Casting: creators are media (Course 804, lesson 5.4)

Creators are media: a channel you cast, brief, buy, and hold to CPA and ROAS. So we cast to persona: who a creator can speak to, the trust they carry, and whether they can deliver an ad.

## Before you start
- Read `04-concepts/personas.md` (each micro persona sheet names a creator type), the mission control file, and any brief in `05-production/briefs/`.
- Ask: how many creators, which personas, which platforms, which countries or languages, budget range, gifting or paid, and the usage rights needed (organic, whitelisting, paid usage window, image and voice).

## Step 1: Search, four ways
Say which you used.

| Way | How | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Warm creators first | People who already commented on, tagged or reviewed the brand | Start here |
| 2. Browser | Search the platform for persona-led keywords and hashtags ("12 hour shift nurse", "cycle to work outfit") | Browse slowly. No scraping at scale |
| 3. Marketplace connector | A connected creator marketplace or database | Use its filters before scoring |
| 4. Scraping tool | Only when the user asks for volume | **Ask for a spending cap first. Stop when you reach it.** Say what each run cost |

A long list is not a shortlist. Only scored creators go into outreach.

**Don't infer personal attributes.** Score what is in the content: what they talk about, who they talk to, how they film. Never label a creator's gender, age, ethnicity or health from a profile. If the brief needs a specific kind of person on camera, flag it and let a person decide.

## Step 2: Score each creator (1 to 5)
| Criterion | What we look for in the content |
|---|---|
| Persona fit | They live the persona's situation, or clearly talk to that persona |
| Talking to camera | Natural, clear, holds attention in the first three seconds |
| Audio and lighting | Usable without a reshoot |
| Content fit | Already makes the kind of content the brief needs |
| Brand safety | Nothing that conflicts with the do's and don'ts |
| Ad readiness | Has done paid or branded work, or clearly could |

Don't rank on follower count.

## Step 3: Tracker
Add each creator to the tracker (Google Sheet, Airtable or Notion through its connector, or `05-production/creators/tracker.csv`): name, handle, link, platform, country, persona, scores, total, one-line why, example video link, status `shortlisted`.

## Step 4: Draft outreach
For the top creators, draft a short, personal message: one line that shows you watched a specific video, why they fit, the ask with fee and usage rights exactly as the user gave them, and a clear next step.

**Agents prepare. People approve.** Never send a message. A person approves and sends every one. Don't negotiate or agree to terms for the brand.

## Step 5 (optional): Reply check-in
If the user wants it, set up a scheduled check-in that reads replies in the outreach inbox and updates each status in the tracker: `contacted`, `replied`, `rate received`, `agreed`, `declined`. It only reads and updates the tracker. It never replies. Give it read access to that one inbox, nothing more.

## Output
A shortlist table in chat (top 10 with scores and reasons), the updated tracker, and outreach drafts.

End every run with **"What's waiting on you"**: a rate to decide, a message to approve, an access problem.

Suggest `aca-creator-brief` for whoever says yes.
