# Play: modular remixing

**What it does:** Builds new ads from proven parts: hook of A + body of B + CTA of C. Part of the three-part winner playbook.
**Choose it when:** Several ads each have a strong part.
**Don't choose it when:** The parts speak to different personas or make different promises.

## Steps
1. List the strong parts with their evidence: hooks by hook rate, bodies by hold rate, CTAs by CTR, all read the team's way.
2. Propose combinations. For each, check that persona, promise and offer still line up.
3. Note what an editor needs: source files, timestamps, any new super.

## Output
A table (`remix | hook from | body from | CTA from | why`) and one iteration brief per remix.

## Checks
- The join between parts makes sense to a first-time viewer.
- Rights cover reusing each creator's footage this way. A person confirms.
