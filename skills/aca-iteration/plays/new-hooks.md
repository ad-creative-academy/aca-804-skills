# Play: new hooks

**What it does:** Keeps the body and CTA and writes 8 to 10 new hooks. Change the hook to change the audience.
**Choose it when:** The story holds and converts, and the hook rate is weak. Or a winner needs to reach new personas.
**Don't choose it when:** The body is the problem.

## Steps
1. Lock the body and CTA. They don't change.
2. Run the `aca-hook-factory` steps for a winner, across at least four plays.
3. Aim some hooks at a different micro persona from `04-concepts/personas.md`.
4. Make every hook visually different. A new line over the same shot is too similar.

## Output
A hook table, plus one iteration brief that covers the set.

## Checks
- Five elements on every hook: persona, message, visual, sound, thumbnail.
- Each hook still leads into the unchanged body.
