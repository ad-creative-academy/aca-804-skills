# Play: bigger visual swing

**What it does:** Keeps the concept and message, and changes the look completely: new scene, location or setup.
**Choose it when:** Small tweaks did nothing, or the platform treats versions as the same ad.
**Don't choose it when:** The ad hasn't earned an iteration yet.

## Steps
1. Write down what must stay: persona, promise, key message points, offer.
2. Propose two or three new worlds for it: a different place, time of day, framing or setup. Take them from the persona's real life in customer voice.
3. Say what each needs to make: a shoot, a creator, or a static through `aca-static-concept` to test the look cheaply first.

## Output
One iteration brief per swing.

## Checks
- Side by side with the original, the first frame is clearly a different ad.
- It still fits the brand's do's and don'ts.
