# Play: format variation

**What it does:** Takes the same concept into a different format or ad type. Part of the three-part winner playbook.
**Choose it when:** A winning concept lives in only one format.
**Don't choose it when:** The audit shows the format itself is why it works.

## Steps
1. State the concept apart from its format: persona, motivator, offer. A concept is not a format.
2. Pick one to three new ad types from `02-language/ad-types.md` that fit the awareness level.
3. Check the concept matrix. If the square already exists and was tested, say so.
4. For a static, hand the row to `aca-static-concept`. For video, to `aca-script-draft`.

## Output
The new matrix rows and the iteration brief.

## Checks
- The promise and offer stay the same.
- The new format looks clearly different in the feed.
