# Hook factory (Course 804, lesson 4.2)

The hook framework comes from the ideation module (401). This is a playbook skill: several plays, and a guide for choosing.

## Before you start, read
- `when-to-use.md` (always)
- the row in `04-concepts/concept-matrix.md`
- `03-research/audience/customer-voice.md` and `04-concepts/personas.md`
- `01-brand/dos-and-donts.md` and `02-language/hook-categories.md` (the team's own categories win over the plays here)

## How many
| Situation | Hooks | What stays fixed |
|---|---|---|
| New concept | 3 | Nothing yet |
| Winner | 10 | Body and CTA stay exactly as they are |

For a winner, changing the hook is how you change the audience.

## Steps
1. Confirm the concept: persona, angle, awareness level, ad type, offer. If one is missing, ask.
2. Read `when-to-use.md` and choose the plays. **Start your answer by saying which plays you chose and why**, in one or two lines.
3. Open each chosen `plays/<name>.md` and follow it.
4. Write each hook with all five elements:

| Element | What to write |
|---|---|
| Persona | Who should stop |
| Message | The spoken line or on-screen line |
| Visual | What is on screen in the first second |
| Sound | The ear stopper: the first sound, voice or audio cue |
| Thumbnail | The still frame someone sees before it plays |

5. Add the pattern interrupt it leans on (curiosity, emotion, relatable, shocking, opportunity) and the play used.
6. Any verbatim customer line carries its source ID (for example R001).

## Review every hook
Ask the three hook questions and answer them plainly:
1. Is it visually appealing?
2. Does it speak to the target?
3. Is it clear?

Then check the set: are the hooks **visually different enough** from each other? Three lines over the same shot are one hook. Change the scene, the person, the framing or the opening action. Small tweaks read as the same ad to the platform.

## Output
A table: `# | play | persona | message | visual | sound | thumbnail | source ID | review notes`. Save to `05-production/hooks/<concept>.md`.

Be direct about weak ones. If a hook fails a review question, say so and offer a fix.

**Show the hooks and stop. Don't write scripts or briefs from them until the strategist picks.** Then suggest `aca-script-draft`.

## Rules
- **Agents prepare. People approve.** Nothing goes to a creator, an editor or an ad account from this skill.
- Follow the do's and don'ts in the mission control file. No banned words or claims.
- Never invent a customer quote, a number or a result.
- If no play fits, ask. Don't invent a new play. New plays come from the strategist.
