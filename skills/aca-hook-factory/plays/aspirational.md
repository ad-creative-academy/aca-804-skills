# Play: aspirational

**What it does:** Opens on the life or feeling the persona wants.
**Choose it when:** The motivator on the concept map is about who they want to be.
**Don't choose it when:** The account shows that polished creative doesn't spend. Check the audit first.

## Steps
1. Take the wanted feeling from the micro persona sheet.
2. Show it in a real setting from their world.
3. One line that links the feeling to the product, in plain words.

## Checks
- It looks native to the feed.
- Where's the feeling? If you can't name it, rewrite.
