# Play: problem or pain point

**What it does:** Opens on the problem, or on the pain it causes.
**Choose it when:** The persona is problem aware and customer voice has a specific moment.
**Don't choose it when:** It would shame or scare the viewer.

## Steps
1. Pick the moment from `03-research/audience/customer-voice.md`, in the customer's words.
2. Show the moment in the first frame. Don't lecture about it.
3. Keep the line short enough to land in three seconds.

## Checks
- Relatable, not shaming.
- No fear-based health claims.
