# Play: question

**What it does:** Opens with a question about the problem.
**Choose it when:** The persona holds a doubt, or asks the question in comments or reviews.
**Don't choose it when:** The honest answer needs a claim the brand can't make.

## Steps
1. Find the question in the customer's own words, with its source ID.
2. Ask it the way they would, not the way a brand would.
3. The first frame shows who is asking, or the situation that raises it.

## Checks
- A first-time viewer understands the question.
- The body of the ad answers it.
