# Play: result or benefit

**What it does:** Opens on the problem already solved, or on the benefit.
**Choose it when:** The persona is solution aware and there is real evidence of the outcome.
**Don't choose it when:** The result can't be backed up.

## Steps
1. Find the outcome in customer voice or in the brand's approved messaging.
2. Show the after state in the first frame.
3. Say it as a customer would. Keep brand claims inside the do's and don'ts.

## Checks
- The result traces to a source.
- Disclaimers are noted where needed (results may vary).
