# Play: funny

**What it does:** Opens with something that makes the persona smile.
**Choose it when:** The situation is light and relatable, and the brand voice allows humor.
**Don't choose it when:** The pain is sensitive, or the joke is on the viewer.

## Steps
1. Find the funny moment in customer voice. Real moments beat written jokes.
2. Write direction and beats for the first three seconds, not a word-for-word line.
3. Let the sound carry it. The ear stopper matters here.

## Checks
- Nobody is the butt of the joke.
- The product still has a clear place in the ad.
