# Play: product first

**What it does:** Opens on the product itself.
**Choose it when:** The persona is product aware or most aware, or the product is striking on screen.
**Don't choose it when:** The persona doesn't know they have the problem yet.

## Steps
1. Use the right product image or shot from `01-brand/brand-assets/`.
2. Pair it with one line that says what it is or what's new.
3. The sound in the first second should match the product (a click, an unboxing sound).

## Checks
- The product is accurate and in stock.
- The offer, if shown, is allowed by `01-brand/offers.md`.
