# Play: call-out

**What it does:** Names the persona in the first line, so the right person stops.
**Choose it when:** The persona has a clear situation or identity.
**Don't choose it when:** The persona is vague ("women 25 to 45").

## Steps
1. Name the situation, not the demographic ("If you work 12-hour shifts...").
2. The first frame shows that world.
3. Cast to persona. Note the creator type from the micro persona sheet.

## Checks
- Specific, warm, never mocking.
- No personal attributes called out that the platform restricts.
