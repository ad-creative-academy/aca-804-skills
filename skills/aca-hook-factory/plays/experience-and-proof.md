# Play: experience and proof

**What it does:** Opens with a personal experience, a customer quote, or social proof.
**Choose it when:** There is a vivid first-person line, a comment with lots of likes, or proof you can show.
**Don't choose it when:** The line is a health or performance claim, or the proof can't be sourced.

## Steps
1. Pick the line verbatim, with its source ID.
2. Trim it to what can be said in three seconds without changing its meaning.
3. For a comment, show it on screen as a reply and answer it calmly.
4. For social proof, use only numbers that are in the Creative OS.

## Checks
- The quote is real and still true after trimming.
- Nothing invented. No merged quotes.
