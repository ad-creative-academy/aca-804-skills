# Ad types: our common language

An ad type is how a concept gets executed. It is not the concept. The concept is the persona, the motivator and the offer. When the whole team uses the same names for ad types, briefs get shorter, references are easy to find, and results can be compared across concepts.

This is a starter list from the ideation module (401). It is not complete. Add the ad types your team invents, and remove the ones you never use.

## The three points of view
The same message can come from three different voices. Using more than one is a simple way to add creative diversity.

| Point of view | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Brand voice | The brand speaks to the customer. Polished, and it follows the brand guidelines |
| Customer voice | A customer speaks, in their own words. It looks native to the feed and often doesn't look like an ad |
| Expert voice | An authority or a publication speaks. More objective in tone. It needs a real expert or source the brand can name |

## Awareness level to ad types

> **A starting point; replace with your own account's learnings.** This mapping comes from the ideation module (401). Your ad account will tell you what really works at each level for your brand. Update this table after every audit and every performance review.

| Awareness level | The ad's job | Ad types to start with |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Educate or entertain, and point to a problem they haven't noticed yet | VSL, comedy skit, trend, text message, street interview |
| Problem aware | Call out the problem and tease the solution | Before and after, customer review, problem vs solution, founder story, bold claim |
| Solution aware | Explain how the product solves the problem | Problem vs solution, post-its, listicle, key benefits, green screen tutorial, customer review, search, infographic |
| Product aware | Give proof that it beats the alternatives | Social proof mashup, news/media, us vs them, reaction, ASMR, product explainer, comments response, podcast |
| Most aware | Give a reason to act now | Promo, price breakdown, unboxing, reaction, social proof mashup |
