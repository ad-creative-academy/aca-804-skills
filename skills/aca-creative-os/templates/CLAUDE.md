## The rules we never break
- <Brand rule, for example "Never say 'detox'. Never claim '48-hour protection'.">

## Who approves what
**Agents prepare. People approve.**

| What | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Copy, scripts, briefs and storyboards | <strategist> |
| Anything sent to a creator, an editor or a client | <name> |
| Launch sheets and anything that touches the ad account | <media buyer> |
| Spending generation credits over <amount> | <name> |
| Moving or deleting docs in this OS | <name> |

Research, drafts, analysis and filing new material with a clear source need no approval. End every long job with one line: what's waiting on me?
